Admissions

Our top priority is to work with our pupils to help them become confident, mature and well-educated young people, and for them to leave us well equipped for the challenges that they will face in their lives.
Please contact the admissions office if you wish to find out about the range Bursaries and Scholarships available. Deciding on the right school for your son or daughter is hugely important. Myddelton College is a non-selective school, consideration for entry into Myddelton College is given to all boys and girls aged between 4 – 18.

 Registration 

 To apply for a place at Myddelton an application form is to be submitted to our Admissions Team with a non-refundable, non-deductible fee of £300 registration fee.

 Entry Requirements 

 A Level applicants are required to have 5 GCSE’s, at Grade 5 and above.
References must be submitted and verified.

 Deposits 

 UK deposit: £500, payable to secure a place
If an offer of a place is given, an invoice for the deposit and registration fee will be issued for immediate payment. Once paid, the place will be confirmed and a full joining pack will be sent. The deposit will be refunded when the pupil leaves Myddelton College, please see our terms and conditions for further details.

 Visit Us
We welcome prospective parents and children for personal visits, with the opportunity to meet our Headmaster, staff and pupils. If you would like to arrange a visit, please do not hesitate to contact via the form below, or reach out to us via email: admissions@myddeltoncollege.com

 Administration Fees 

 Registration Fee 

 For all UK and International pupils, a non-refundable registration fee of £300 is payable when you register your child for a place at Myddelton College.

 Application Fee 

 For International Pupils an application fee of £150 will also be applicable.

 Deposits 

 Payable to secure a place, to be paid once an offer has been made. Refundable when pupil leaves the school.

 Discounts, Scholarships and Bursaries 

 Sibling Discount 

 A 5% reduction is applied to the fees per additional sibling. The reduction is applied to the eldest of the siblings at the school

 Armed Forces Discount 

 The School offers all those parents who serve, or have served, in the Armed Forces of the Crown a 25% discount off the full School fees. When used in conjunction with the Boarding School Allowance, this makes our boarding fees a very affordable option.

 Scholarships 

 Myddelton College is committed to broadening access to our excellent service by offering financial support with payment to school fees via scholarships and bursaries. 
Scholarships are based entirely on merit and not linked to ability to pay. Scholarships are generally awarded to students entering KS3 (Years 7 – 9), Year 10 and Year 12, but on occasions they are awarded to exceptionally talented students, academic or other, in other years. 

 Bursaries 

 The School is committed to broad access to the School by offering to eligible parents, means-tested financial support with the payment of Schools fees. Such support may be awarded in the form of a discount on the tuition fees payable. Such requests should be made in the first instance to the Head of Admissions.

 Other Awards 

 The School also offer discounts to pupils whose parents are in the Diplomatic Corps (10% of full fees) and of those who have a parent who attended the former Howells School’s (Old Howellians Alumni) (10% of full fees).
