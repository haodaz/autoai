# Elite Sports | Co-educational School Denbigh | Myddelton College

Source: https://www.myddeltoncollege.com/elite-sports/

Elite Sports

 Myddelton College pupils embrace sports and fitness and make use of our 37½ acre campus.
We have a full-size football pitch, rugby pitch, 4 tennis courts, an indoor climbing wall, a large indoor sports hall, a dance studio and gym area as well as a brand new Equestrian facility!
Our pupils benefit from our facilities, improving their physical health as well as their mental wellbeing. During their time with us, your child will participate in activities which promote teamwork and independence, build agility, communication skills and coordination.
The Elite Sports Department offers a wide range of programmes to support pupils’ academic learning and ensure they enjoy their time at the college and are physically fit. The pupils gain a diverse range of experiences that will benefit them physically, socially and develop discipline and leadership skills needed to be successful in the future, be it in further education or employment. The college has many athletes that compete in national teams including triathlon, swimming, gymnastics, horse riding, sailing, climbing and ice skating among others.
We realise it takes a lot of support to help these young athletes reach their full potential. Participation in national events and competitions means they may need to leave college early at times. Our Pupil Development Centre is on hand to give them the academic support they require. And our Microsoft OneNote software enables these pupils to easily catch up with any lessons they have missed.

# Outdoor and Adventure Education | Myddelton College

Source: https://www.myddeltoncollege.com/about-us/adventure-education/

Outdoor and Adventure Education

 Outdoor & Adventure Education
Unlocking Lifelong Independence Through Outdoor & Adventure Education (OAE)
At the heart of Outdoor and Adventure Education is a powerful mission: to cultivate lifelong independence in pupils. We ignite their spirit of adventure by encouraging them to embrace challenges and invest in their personal growth, building unwavering self-confidence along the way.
Our Outdoor and Adventure Education Programme immerses pupils in stunning natural settings where they can make authentic decisions and face real risks. We prioritise holistic transformative experiences that shape who they become, rather than just what they achieve.
Forget rigid curriculums—our flexible approach allows us to adapt teaching to meet individual needs and respond to the dynamic outdoor environment. This fosters spontaneous, experiential learning opportunities that embrace a benefit-risk mindset.
How much Outdoor and Adventure Education is in the school curriculum? 
Prep School: Half a day of OAE every 2 weeks
KS3 (Year 7-9): One whole day of OAE every 2 weeks
Key Objectives:

1. Personal and Social Development: Empowering pupils to enhance confidence, teamwork, interpersonal skills, and independence.
2. Resilience and Mental Well-Being: Fostering a lifelong connection with nature through thrilling adventures.
3. Curriculum Enrichment: Deepening classroom learning with rich, real-world context.
