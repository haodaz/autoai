About Us

 Myddelton College is a top Independent Boarding and Day School in the UK, established on a 37½ acre campus. The College takes its name from the renowned Myddelton family who, dating back to the 15th Century, were eminent figures in the history of Denbighshire and Wales, having been Members of Parliament for the local area as well as contributing to wider society across the United Kingdom. 
Myddelton is located on the outskirts of the historic market town of Denbigh, situated at the foot of the 13th Century fortress of Denbigh Castle which dates back to 1859 and contains the perfect blend of historic architecture with modern facilities. The school is surrounded by a rich history as well as plenty of amenities and the spectacular Welsh countryside. It is easily accessible by road, rail and close to major airports.

The values of Myddelton have been founded upon three key pillars: Scholarship, Fellowship and Resilience.

 Three Pillars of Myddelton 

 Scholarship 

 We instil in our pupils a love and passion for learning. We teach them not to be nervous of academic rigour. We celebrate it and indeed expect to see it in every single lesson.

 Fellowship 

 We place great emphasis on our supportive community and developing a young person with an internationalist outlook. We teach our pupils that the biggest impact they can have on another person’s life is simply by being kind and thoughtful to them. We uphold this value every day at Myddelton College.

 Resilience 

 Our pupils are taught to never give up in the pursuit of goals and achievements. We teach them how to cope with setbacks and learn from them. We help them develop a positive mindset that embraces challenge, makes no excuses, and is never defeated.

 Learning Through Technology
Myddelton College is a Microsoft Global Showcase school and member of the exclusive EdTech50 group. Each pupil at Myddelton College has their own personal tablet device for learning.
All our lessons are delivered through Microsoft OneNote and Teams which provides parents and pupils with access to lessons from anywhere in the world at any time. This means our pupils receive personalised feedback from their teachers in real time. Pupils’ work is then differentiated to help them improve on their own targets, ensuring the work provided is both challenging and accessible to every pupil.
Many of our staff have been recognised for their exceptional abilities to teach in innovative ways using technology. They have been singled out by Microsoft as MIEEs (Microsoft Innovative Educator Experts) ensuring that our staff and pupils are at the cutting edge of classroom learning. We guarantee that pupils are well versed in modern technology and are prepared for the modern workplace.

Academic Results

 Myddelton College takes great pride in its rich history of guiding pupils toward their desired futures, and this year’s Academic Results are no exception. We are thrilled to announce an impressive set of A Level and GCSE results.
A-Level Results
Ranked among the Top 200 co-educational independent schools in the UK, Myddelton College takes great pride in supporting every pupil to achieve their potential and progress onto exciting futures. This year, 80% of A level grades were awarded at A*–C, a reflection of the dedication, resilience, and ambition of our pupils across a wide range of subjects.
Our leavers have secured places at leading universities both in the UK and abroad, with almost a third progressing to Russell Group institutions. They are now studying a breadth of competitive courses, including Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Law, Psychology, Business, Economics, and the Arts. Beyond university, pupils have also embraced alternative pathways: two were awarded prestigious apprenticeships, and one pupil achieved a football scholarship to study in the USA.
These diverse successes highlight the individual strengths and aspirations of our pupils, as well as the tailored guidance and personal support that ensures every pupil at Myddelton finds the right pathway for their future.
